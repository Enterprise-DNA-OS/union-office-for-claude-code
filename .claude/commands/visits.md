# visits

Run `npm run union -- visits`. Fill in the operator's values. Add `--json` when you need every field.

AU visits are checked for an entry notice given 1 to 14 days ahead and a permit current on the day. Offer `draft-entry-notice <visit>` for any visit without a notice.

Nothing here sends a message or contacts anyone.
