# Why there is no front end

A union office runs on a handful of records: members, workplaces, delegates, dues, cases, agreements and visits. The screens on top of them are there because the database was hard to ask. It is not hard any more. Open this folder in Claude Code and ask "which grievances run out this month?" or "who did Kauri leave off the October remittance?" and you get the answer from your own records.

## What you gain

- **Answers to your own questions**, not only the reports someone built.
- **No per-seat licence.** Every organiser and officer can ask.
- **Your records in a database you own.** Plain tables. Back them up, query them from anything, leave any time.
- **Rules that match your rule book.** Change a rule in plain language with `/customise`.

## What a screen gives that this does not

- **A member portal and online payments.** Members cannot log in here. Keep your payment provider, or have Enterprise DNA build the portal into your version.
- **A phone app for organisers on site.** It runs where Claude Code runs. Organisers log a visit afterwards, or Enterprise DNA adds a phone screen.
- **Drag and drop.** Cases and bargaining rounds change state by asking, not by dragging cards.

`npm run view` renders read-only pages (the week, dues, organising) in your colours for anyone who wants to look rather than ask.
